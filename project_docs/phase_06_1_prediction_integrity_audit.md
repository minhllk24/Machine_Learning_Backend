## 2. Forensic Investigation of Reported Discrepancies

The following discrepancies identified in previous narrative reports were individually investigated against the physical parquet prediction records:

### 2.1. CatBoost Tweedie Primary WAPE: 73.51% (Phase 5.1) vs. 71.74% (Phase 6 Table 4.1)
- **Investigation:**
  - In `artifacts/predictions/tree_models_oof_predictions.parquet` (68,680 Primary rows for `CatBoost__Tweedie_p15`), total actual demand $\sum y = 2,025,137.0$, and total absolute error $\sum |y - \hat{y}| = 1,488,713.8410$.
  - Recomputed Pooled WAPE $= 1,488,713.8410 / 2,025,137.0 = \mathbf{0.735118}$ (**73.51%**).
  - This matches `artifacts/tables/tree_models_metrics_pooled_oof.csv` row 7 exactly.
  - Inspection of `artifacts/tables/tree_models_metrics_by_fold.csv` row 7 reveals: **Fold 2 WAPE = 71.7483%**.
- **Root Cause Classification:** **`REPORTING_OR_TRANSCRIPTION_ERROR`**.
  The author of Table 4.1 in `project_docs/phase_06_deep_model_report.md` inadvertently transcribed the Fold 2 WAPE figure (71.74%) into the pooled summary row instead of the canonical pooled figure (73.51%).
- **Canonical Value:** **73.51% WAPE** (MAE 21.6761, RMSE 77.7319, Signed Bias -5.02%).
